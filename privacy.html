Application Scope Notice: This Privacy Policy applies strictly and exclusively to the mobile game application "Gem Atelier" (available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store). 1. Introduction

Welcome to Gem Atelier ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have regarding your data when you play Gem Atelier on mobile devices.

2. Information We Collect

Gem Atelier is designed as a standalone casual puzzle game. We do not require user registration, and we do not collect personal identifying information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, phone number, email address, or government-issued IDs.

We may automatically collect limited technical and non-identifiable gameplay data:

  • Device & System Data: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, preferred language locale, and battery/network connection status.
  • Anonymous Identifiers: Standard advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID on Android, and Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA on iOS subject to App Tracking Transparency permissions).
  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Completed level numbers, scores, virtual coin balances, game settings (sound, music, haptics), and session duration stored locally on your device.
  • Crash & Performance Reports: Diagnostic reports and stack traces generated when the application encounters an error, used solely for resolving bugs and improving stability.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Game integrates selected third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, and platform billing. These services may collect information as outlined in their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used for displaying interstitial and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads in accordance with your consent choices. You can learn more about Google's data practices at Google Ads Privacy Policy.
  • Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit) & Google Play Billing: When you purchase virtual items or "Remove Ads", financial transactions are handled directly by Apple or Google. We never receive or store your credit card or financial account numbers.

4. In-App Purchases & "Remove Ads"

Gem Atelier offers an optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase. When purchased, all non-voluntary interstitial and banner ads are permanently disabled. Purchase verification and entitlement restoration are processed securely through Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Gem Atelier is an all-ages family-friendly puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such data.

6. Data Security & Storage

Your gameplay progress and local preferences are stored directly on your mobile device. Any aggregated diagnostic telemetry is transmitted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.

7. Your Rights & Opt-Out Options

You have full control over advertising identifiers and tracking:

  • iOS Users: You can manage or revoke tracking permissions via Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Gem Atelier.
  • Android Users: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads via Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Data Deletion: Because we do not maintain accounts on our servers, uninstalling the Game immediately deletes all locally stored gameplay data from your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal regulations. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
